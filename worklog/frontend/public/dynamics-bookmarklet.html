📊 Dynamics 365 Sync Bookmarklet

This bookmarklet allows you to sync time entries between your Worklog app and Microsoft Dynamics 365 without needing API tokens.

⚠️ Important: This bookmarklet works by running JavaScript in your Dynamics tab, using your existing browser session. It's completely safe and doesn't store or transmit your credentials anywhere.

📥 Installation

Step 1: Drag this button to your bookmarks bar:

🔄 Sync Dynamics

If you can't drag it, right-click → "Add to Bookmarks" or "Bookmark This Link"

🔑 Get Your JWT Token

Step 2: Get your JWT token from your Worklog app:
  1. Open your Worklog app at http://localhost:3001
  2. Make sure you're logged in
  3. Open Developer Tools (F12)
  4. Go to Console tab
  5. Paste this code and press Enter:
console.log(localStorage.getItem('jwt_token'))
  1. Copy the token that appears (WITHOUT the quotes)
  2. Should look like: eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9...
  3. DO NOT include the quote marks " "

🔍 Find Your Bookable Resource ID

Method 1: Using Browser Console (Easiest)
  1. Open your Dynamics 365 time entries page
  2. Open Developer Tools (F12) → Console tab
  3. Paste this code and press Enter:
fetch(window.location.origin + '/api/data/v9.0/msdyn_timeentries?$select=_msdyn_bookableresource_value&$top=1').then(r => r.json()).then(d => console.log('Your Resource ID:', d.value[0]?._msdyn_bookableresource_value))
  1. Copy the GUID that appears (format: a1b2c3d4-e5f6-7890-abcd-ef1234567890)
  2. Go to Settings in your Worklog app and paste it
Method 2: From Time Entry URL
  1. Open any of YOUR time entries in Dynamics (click to edit one)
  2. Look at the URL in your browser
  3. Find "_msdyn_bookableresource_value=" in the URL
  4. Copy the GUID after the equals sign
Method 3: Ask Your Admin

If the above methods don't work, ask your Dynamics administrator for your Bookable Resource ID.

🚀 Usage

For IMPORTING from Dynamics to Worklog:
  1. Open your Dynamics 365 time entries page
  2. Click the "🔄 Sync Dynamics" bookmarklet in your bookmarks bar
  3. Paste your JWT token when prompted
  4. Click OK when asked (OK = Import)
  5. Enter the date range
  6. Wait for import to complete
For EXPORTING from Worklog to Dynamics:
  1. Open your Dynamics 365 time entries page
  2. Click the "🔄 Sync Dynamics" bookmarklet in your bookmarks bar
  3. Paste your JWT token when prompted
  4. Click Cancel when asked (Cancel = Export)
  5. Enter the date range
  6. Wait for export to complete

📝 Notes

  • The bookmarklet uses your existing Dynamics session (cookies), so no Bearer token is needed
  • Imported entries are assigned to your default project - you may need to update them
  • The sync is manual - click the bookmarklet each time you want to sync
  • Duplicates are automatically skipped based on date and Dynamics ID
  • All console output is visible in Developer Tools (F12) for debugging
🔒 Security Note: Your JWT token gives access to your Worklog account. Keep it private and don't share it. The token expires after 24 hours.

🐛 Troubleshooting

  • Script not loading: Make sure your Worklog app is running at http://localhost:3001
  • CORS errors: The script must be served from the same domain as your Worklog app
  • Authentication errors: Your JWT token may have expired - get a new one
  • No entries imported: Check the date range and console logs (F12)
